privilegedAccess

PARTIAL Nobody holds Global Administrator permanently except the emergency accounts. An account other than the emergency accounts holds Global Administrator permanently.

Global Administrator can do anything in the tenant, so an account holding it around the clock is the most valuable target there is, and it is dangerous every hour of every day rather than only while someone is using it. The emergency accounts are the deliberate exception: they exist precisely to always work. Everyone else should hold the role only while doing the work that needs it.

Control privileged-001

authorizationPolicy

FULL Guests do not get the same view of the directory as your own staff. Matches the standard.

A guest is somebody outside your organisation who was invited in, often for one project and often long ago. Giving them the same view of the directory as an employee hands them your staff list, your group structure and who reports to whom, which is the raw material for a convincing phishing message. Two settings below member level are acceptable: limited, which is the default, and restricted, which is tighter still. Restricted is the better of the two but it stops working with some services, so it is not required here.

Control apps-001

PARTIAL People cannot grant an application any permission it asks for. People can approve applications for themselves. If this tenant is on the setting Microsoft manages for you, which is the defaul...

Consent phishing does not steal a password. It shows the person a real sign in page and asks them to approve an application, and once approved that application keeps its access even after the password changes and the second step is passed. Two answers pass: turning user consent off entirely, or limiting it to low impact permissions from applications whose publisher has been verified. Everything else fails, including the setting Microsoft manages on your behalf, because that one still lets people approve applications and only holds back a list of permissions Microsoft maintains and can change. A setting this tool has never seen fails too, since a permission that lets people approve applications is not one to assume the best about.

What is not covered:

  • People can approve applications for themselves. If this tenant is on the setting Microsoft manages for you, which is the default for tenants created recently, then the most sensitive permissions such as mail, files and calendars are held back and everything else is still theirs to approve. That is better than the old setting and it is not the same as deciding who may approve what.

Control tenants-001

PARTIAL People who are not administrators cannot read the recovery keys for their own devices. Anybody can retrieve the disk recovery key for a device they own.

Disk encryption is what protects a laptop once it is out of the hands of the person carrying it. It is in the stricter set because it puts recovery through a help desk, which is a real cost.
